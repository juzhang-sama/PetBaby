# pixel-style-v1 提示词合同

这是 PetBaby 的固定像素视觉语言，不是单次生成的可选风格。生成必须输出一张完整、透明背景的宠物 PNG，并把照片里可辨认的身份特征映射到同一只宠物上——辨识度优先于细节，追求「一眼就认出来」。

## 标准模板形象（最高优先级，不可修改）

The pet MUST follow this STANDARD TEMPLATE FORM, regardless of the pose, angle, body shape or proportions shown in the input photo(s):

- 大头 Q 版：the head is extra-large and rounded, dominating the sprite — about 1.8 heads tall, noticeably larger than the body
- 标准规范形态：front-facing, upright and centered, short round body with small stubby paws, tail curled naturally beside the body
- 构图：full body visible, no cropping or touching the canvas edges, at least 4% transparent margin on all sides
- 只输出一张完整宠物图，不输出零件、图层或骨架

CRITICAL: The input photo contributes ONLY the identity — face shape, ear shape, eye color, fur colors, coat patterns and markings. The BODY SHAPE and POSE come EXCLUSIVELY from the template above. DO NOT reproduce the pose, the lying-down position, or the body proportions seen in the photo.

## Style

16-bit pixel art game sprite. Chunky clearly-visible pixels with a medium pixel-grid density. Hard edges with no anti-aliasing. Limited color palette with flat shading and subtle dithering. Bold dark outlines around every feature. Retro 16-bit game sprite charm.

## Identity（一眼认出）

The subject must be INSTANTLY recognizable at a glance. Preserve these identity features faithfully from the photo: fur colors, coat patterns and markings, face shape, ear shape, eye color, chest fur, paw socks, body patches, tail shape and markings, and signature marks. Recognition comes from these key features, not from realism — do NOT add microscopic fur texture or smooth gradients.

## Forbidden

smooth gradients, soft shading, anti-aliasing, photorealism, fur texture, tiny low-resolution sprite, mosaic filter, generic cat replacement, face patch compositing, body-part assembly, semantic layers, Live2D rig, AND copying the pose or body proportions of the input photo.
